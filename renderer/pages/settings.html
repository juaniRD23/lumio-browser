Import from another browser

Bookmarks, history and passwords
Copy bookmarks, the last 90 days of history and, from Chrome-based browsers on a Mac, saved passwords. From Safari, export your passwords (File › Export › Passwords) and import the file below.

Turn on Lumio Browser under Full Disk Access in System Settings, then import again, or use files exported from Safari.
