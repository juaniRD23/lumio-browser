Recovery key

Your recovery key
Your synced data is encrypted on your devices: Lumio can’t read it. Write this key down somewhere safe. With it, you can set up a new device even if your others are lost.

Workflows

Teach Lumio a task once
After Lumio finishes a task, click “Save as workflow” under its answer, or ask it to save one. Then run it again by typing / in the chat box, from the new tab page, or on a schedule.

Site tips

Lumio learns shortcuts
When Lumio figures out a faster way to do something on a site, it saves a short tip here and uses it the next time. Tips stay on this computer and never include personal details.

Scheduled tasks

New scheduled task
Lumio does it on its own at the time you pick, in a new chat, and lets you know when it’s done. You can also just ask Lumio, like “every weekday at 8, summarize the top tech news.”

Scheduled tasks run while Lumio Browser is open (a run missed in the last 6 hours happens when you open it) and use your plan’s weekly allowance. In Ask mode, Lumio still asks before it acts and sends you a notification.

Import from another browser

Bookmarks, history and passwords
Copy bookmarks, the last 90 days of history and, from Chrome-based browsers on a Mac, saved passwords. From Safari, export your passwords (File › Export › Passwords) and import the file below.
