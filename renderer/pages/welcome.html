Keep your passwords safe

Lumio locks your saved passwords, passkeys and sign-ins with a key in your Mac’s Keychain. When you continue, macOS asks for permission once.

Lumio Browser wants to use your confidential information stored in “Lumio Browser Safe Storage” in your keychain. To allow this, enter the “login” keychain password.

Bring your stuff over

Copy your bookmarks, history and passwords from the browser you use now.

Lumio needs permission to read Safari. macOS protects Safari’s bookmarks and history. In System Settings, turn on Lumio Browser under Full Disk Access, then try again. Or use files exported from Safari below.

Or import a file:

From Safari: choose File › Export › Bookmarks and File › Export › Passwords, then pick the files here.

Choose your search engine
