Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what Lumio (run by Fifty Sites) collects and why: the website, Lumio Chat, Lumio Browser on your computer and Lumio for iPhone and iPad. We don’t sell your data, use it for advertising or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

What we collect

  • Account: from Sign in with Apple or Google sign-in, your name, email address (with Apple, this can be a private relay address Apple makes for you), your Apple or Google account ID and, with Google, your profile picture. If you sign up with your email address and a password, we keep your email address, and your password only as a salted one-way hash (PBKDF2), never the password itself. The sign-in codes we email you are stored only as a hash and expire after 15 minutes. Resend sends Lumio’s sign-in emails (your email address and the code). To stop abuse of sign-in, a one-way hash of your network address is kept for one hour, and a one-way hash of your email address for up to a day.
  • Lumio Chat: your chats and messages, the files you attach (pictures, and the text of documents) and the pictures and documents Lumio makes for you, so you can come back to them. Deleting a chat deletes its messages and files. Attachments you never send are deleted after a day.
  • Usage: when you use Lumio AI and what it cost, to apply your plan’s allowance.
  • Billing: Stripe processes payments. We keep your Stripe customer ID and plan status; we never see or store your card number.
  • Issue reports: when you use Help › Report an issue in Lumio Browser, we keep what you wrote and only what you chose to include (an email to reply to, the page’s address, a screenshot, your Lumio and system versions) for up to 180 days. To stop abuse, a one-way hash of your network address is kept with it for one hour.

Lumio Browser and Lumio for iPhone and iPad

Your browsing history, bookmarks, passwords, chats with Lumio AI in the browser and settings stay on your device unless Lumio Sync is on (below). When you ask Lumio AI to help, the messages, voice clips, page content and screenshots it needs for that request are sent to our server and the AI model to answer; we don’t keep browser page content or voice clips after the request, except a short-lived copy used to avoid charging twice for a retried step. On iPhone and iPad, Lumio AI asks before it sends anything the first time, and you can turn that off again in Settings › Lumio AI.

Websites you visit can ask to use your camera, microphone or location, or to reach devices on your network; your device asks you first, and what you allow goes to that website, not to us. Lumio uses the microphone itself only while you hold the mic button to talk to Lumio AI.

Crash reports are off unless you turn them on (Settings › Privacy, or the welcome screens). Then, when Lumio Browser crashes, it sends us a report to help fix the problem: the Lumio version, your system type (Mac, Windows or Linux, and the processor type) and technical details of the crash. A crash snapshot (minidump) can contain bits of what was in Lumio’s memory at that moment. Reports aren’t linked to your account. We keep them for 90 days, and we keep a scrambled form of your IP address for one day only, to limit how many reports one network can send.

Lumio Sync and your computer

Lumio Sync is on when you sign in to Lumio Browser or Lumio for iPhone and iPad, and you can turn it off in Settings › Sync. What you choose to sync (such as bookmarks, history, chats, settings, open tabs, and passwords between computers) is encrypted on your device with your account’s sync key before it’s uploaded. Payment methods sync only if you turn them on.

How the sync key is kept. By default, our server also keeps a copy of your sync key, encrypted with a key only our server has, so signing in is all a new device needs to start syncing, like Google Chrome’s default sync. This means Lumio could technically decrypt your synced data. We don’t sell it, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models. When a device gets the key, a one-way hash of its network address is kept for one hour to stop abuse. Signing out of Lumio on a device removes the sync key from that device.

Encrypt with your own passphrase. In Lumio Browser, Settings › Sync › Advanced, you can choose to keep the sync key only on your devices. We then delete our copy, and your synced data starts over with a new key that never reaches our server, so we can’t read it. New devices then need your approval on a device that already syncs, or your recovery key; if you lose all of them, we can’t recover your synced data. A deleted copy of a key can stay in our database’s backups for up to 30 days.

We keep the encrypted copies, a list of your devices with their names and random IDs, and our encrypted copy of your sync key until you reset sync or delete your synced data (Settings › Sync), or delete your account. Tasks and tabs you send between your phone and Lumio on your computer are encrypted the same way.

If you turn on notifications on iPhone or iPad (Settings › Your Computer), we keep the push token Apple gives your device so we can tell it that Lumio on your computer finished a task or needs your OK. The notification only says what kind of thing happened; the details stay encrypted. Turning notifications off, or deleting your account, removes the token.

Connected apps

If you connect Google (Drive, Gmail, Calendar) or Microsoft (Outlook, OneDrive), Lumio gets read-only access. It reads your email, calendar or files only when you ask it to in a chat, and only what it needs to answer. The access tokens are stored encrypted. We don’t use this data for anything else, don’t sell it, and don’t use it to train AI models. Content Lumio reads becomes part of that chat (so you can see what it used) until you delete the chat. You can disconnect anytime on your account page, and also remove Lumio’s access in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

Lumio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI processing

To answer, your messages (and any files, voice clips, page content, screenshots or connected-app content they include) go to our AI model provider (OpenRouter, which routes to the model’s maker, such as OpenAI, Google or Anthropic). We only use providers that don’t keep or train on what you send. They process it to produce the reply under their own terms and policies.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can delete chats anytime. You can delete your account and its data yourself in Lumio for iPhone and iPad (Settings › your account › Delete Account), or contact us (see below) from the email you signed in with. Deleting your account ends any paid plan, revokes Lumio’s access to your Apple or Google sign-in and connected apps, and deletes your chats, files, synced data, sync key, devices and push tokens from our servers. Records of how much Lumio AI you used are kept without your account or any content, for our accounting.
