Support

Help with Lumio Browser, Lumio Chat, Lumio for iPhone and iPad, and Lumio plans. Lumio is run by Fifty Sites.

Contact us

  • Help, billing, privacy and legal: support@lumio-co.online. If it’s about your account, write from the email you signed in with.
  • Anything else: hello@lumio-co.online.

Emails Lumio sends you, like sign-in codes, come from no-reply@lumio-co.online and can’t take replies. Write to one of the addresses above instead.

Your plan

You can change plans or cancel anytime from your account (Manage billing). Charges may appear from “Fifty Sites”. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep your plan until the end of the paid month.

Delete your account

  • In Lumio for iPhone and iPad: Settings › your account › Delete Account.
  • Or email support@lumio-co.online from the email you signed in with and ask us to delete it.

Deleting your account ends any paid plan, revokes Lumio’s access to your Apple or Google sign-in and connected apps, and deletes your chats, files, synced data, sync key, devices and push tokens from our servers. The Privacy Policy says what’s kept.

Using Lumio

  • Lumio on your phone: open lumio-co.online/companion on your phone and sign in to the same Lumio account as Lumio Browser.
  • Report a problem from Lumio Browser: Help › Report an issue. You choose what to include.
  • Common questions: see Good to know.
